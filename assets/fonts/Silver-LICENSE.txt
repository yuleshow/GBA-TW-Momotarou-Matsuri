Project adaptation: Silver is rasterized at 11pt with grayscale coverage,
then thresholded into monochrome 8x8 GBA glyph tiles (normally at 96/255,
or the glyph's peak coverage for tiny marks). This is a reduced-size adaptation, not the
font's native pixel size. The supplied TTF is not modified. No endorsement
by the font authors is implied. Retain this attribution with distributed patches.
